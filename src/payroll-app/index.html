Timesheet & computation

Monthly rate and the timesheet cells are editable — every figure to the right (daily/hourly rate, OT, SSS, PhilHealth, HDMF, withholding tax and net pay) recomputes the moment you leave the cell. Overtime is split by the three rates in the process document — 1.25× regular workday, 1.69× rest day or special non-working, 2.60× regular holiday. ND hrs are hours worked 10:00PM–6:00AM, paid at hourly × 10% (auto-suggested as OT hours beyond the first 3, editable). Transp. allow. is entered directly as an amount. De minimis is not entered at all — it is computed every cutoff as the standard ₱1,500, prorated by working days when someone's start date falls inside the cutoff. Both are non-taxable. Managers are exempt from OT, night differential and holiday premium.

Other expenses, allowances & deductions

Add recurring items like Maxicare HMO, rice allowance, company loans or MP2. Choose whether the item hits every payout, only the 1st–15th cutoff, or only the 16th–EOM cutoff.

Loans

Enter a loan once and it amortises itself — the installment number and remaining balance are derived by counting payouts from the start date, so correcting a start date reflows the whole schedule. Deduction stops automatically when the balance reaches zero, and a final short installment is truncated to whatever is left.

Holiday calendar

Only holidays fixed by law are pre-loaded. Movable holidays — Holy Week, Eid'l Fitr, Eid'l Adha, National Heroes Day and any special days added by proclamation — need to be entered here once Malacañang releases the proclamation for the year.

13th month pay & annualised tax

13th month is total basic salary earned in the year divided by 12 — absences reduce it, paid leave does not. The adjustment compares tax actually withheld across the year against the tax due on the annualised income: a positive figure is refundable to the employee, a negative one is still collectible.

Computation rules & assumptions

  1. Pay periods. The 25th pays the 1st–15th of the same month. The 10th pays the 16th to the end of the previous month.
  2. Schedule. Monday–Friday, 10:00AM–7:00PM, 1-hour unpaid break, 8 paid hours a day. Saturday and Sunday are rest days.
  3. Overtime. Credited for hours worked after 7:00PM. Regular workday 1.25×; rest day or special non-working day 1.69× (130% base × 130% OT premium); regular holiday 2.60× (200% base × 130% OT premium).
  4. Night differential. Hourly rate × 10% × night hours worked, for hours between 10:00PM and 6:00AM. The 10% is computed on the base hourly rate, per the process document.
  5. Transportation allowance. Entered directly as an amount per cutoff, non-taxable.
  6. De minimis. Computed automatically, never encoded: the standard ₱1,500 per cutoff, non-taxable, prorated by working days when a start date falls inside the cutoff. Someone hired after the cutoff closed gets none. Change the standard amount in Settings.
  7. Contributions. SSS from the 2026 table (Regular EE + MPF EE); PhilHealth 2.5% employee and 2.5% employer; HDMF a flat ₱200 each side. Each is deducted whole on its own payout: SSS on the 25th (pay period 2), PhilHealth and HDMF on the 10th (pay period 1).
  8. Managers. Treated as managerial staff exempt under Art. 82 of the Labor Code — no overtime, night differential, rest day or holiday premium.
  9. Daily rate. Monthly × 12 ÷ 261 working days (Mon–Fri with regular holidays paid). Change the factor above if your company uses 313, 314 or 365.
  10. Contributions. SSS, PhilHealth and HDMF are monthly amounts based on the monthly basic rate, split in half across the two payouts by default.
  11. Tax. Withholding is computed per payout on gross taxable pay less the employee's statutory contributions. Non-taxable allowances (rice, de minimis) are excluded.
  12. Not included. 13th month pay, final pay, leave conversion and the BIR 2316 annualisation are outside this tool's scope.

Check before live use. Statutory rates change by circular. Verify the SSS contribution schedule, the PhilHealth premium rate and the HDMF ceiling against the current issuances, and have the setup reviewed by your accountant before running real payroll.
